---

### 2. Every Term on Slide 1 Explained in Plain Language:

* **MoSJE (Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment):** The Government of India ministry responsible for the welfare, educational upliftment, and economic empowerment of Scheduled Castes (SC), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Senior Citizens, and Persons with Disabilities.
* **MSME (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises):** Government categorization for businesses. A **Micro-enterprise** is an enterprise where investment in plant and machinery does not exceed ₹1 Crore and annual turnover does not exceed ₹5 Crores.
* **Credit Deficit (₹25 Lakh Crore Gap):** The massive difference between the total loan capital micro-entrepreneurs *need* to grow their business and what formal commercial banks actually lend them. Because formal banks reject them, borrowers are pushed to local loan sharks charging 36% to 60% interest per year.
* **Information Asymmetry:** A situation where one party (government policy makers) has all the scheme details, but the intended beneficiary (rural artisan) has zero awareness of what they are entitled to or how to apply.
* **The "DPR Wall":** When a poor borrower approaches a bank branch, the bank manager asks for a "Detailed Project Report" showing cash flows and balance sheets. Since a street vendor or cobbler cannot hire a chartered accountant to make balance sheets, the manager rejects the application on day one. This rejection barrier is called the "DPR Wall".
* **Concessional Credit:** Loans provided at heavily subsidized, below-market interest rates (e.g., 4.0% p.a. under NSFDC compared to 14%–18% standard commercial unsecured business loans).
* **Capital Subsidy Grant:** Direct non-repayable financial grant funded by the government (e.g., 25% under NSFDC, up to 35% under PMEGP). If you take a ₹3 Lakh loan with a 25% subsidy, the government gives you ₹75,000 as a free grant, and you only have to repay ₹2,25,000.

### 2. Every Term on Slide 3 Explained in Plain Language:

* **FastAPI:** A modern, ultra-high-speed Python backend web framework designed for asynchronous REST APIs and microservices.
* **Next.js PWA (Progressive Web App):** A modern web application that works on any browser but can be installed on a mobile phone like a native Android app, caching assets to work smoothly on slow 2G/3G rural networks.
* **Verhoeff Checksum Algorithm:** A mathematical error-detection formula based on abstract algebra (dihedral group $D_5$). UIDAI uses it to verify whether a 12-digit Aadhaar number is mathematically valid before hitting government servers.
* **OpenCV Laplacian Variance Filter:** An image-processing algorithm that measures the rate of change of pixel intensity (edges). If an image has sharp text, the variance of the Laplacian is high ($>100$). If a document photo is blurry, the score is low ($<100$), allowing our system to reject blurry photos before submission.
* **KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission):** A statutory body under the Ministry of MSME that publishes official standard cost templates ("Model Project Profiles") for 200+ rural businesses detailing machinery costs, raw material costs, and expected profit margins.
* **mTLS (Mutual Transport Layer Security):** An ultra-secure enterprise communication standard where both the client and the bank server verify each other's cryptographic certificates before sending data.
* **SCA (State Channelizing Agency):** State government corporations (such as the UP SC/ST Development Corporation) that act as the state-level nodal agency to verify applicant eligibility before forwarding loan files to commercial banks.
